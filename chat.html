At FiTFEAST, I believe that healthy eating should be simple, enjoyable, and personalized for every individual.

I developed FiTFEAST as a personalized meal recommendation system to help users make healthier food choices based on their dietary preferences, nutritional requirements, and fitness goals. By combining user-specific inputs with intelligent recommendation techniques, the system generates meal suggestions that support a balanced lifestyle. Whether the goal is improving fitness, maintaining a healthy diet, or exploring nutritious meal options, FiTFEAST provides tailored recommendations to make healthy eating more accessible and convenient.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Users face challenges finding suitable recipes due to dietary restrictions, health concerns (e.g., allergies), and diverse ingredient preparation methods. Recommender systems address this by filtering options based on preferences, nutritional needs, and constraints, making healthy, personalized eating simpler and more convenient.

SOLUTION STATEMENT

FitFeast uses a smart recommender system to provide personalized meal recommendations based on dietary restrictions, health concerns, and lifestyle goals. It simplifies healthy eating by offering recipes that align with individual needs while highlighting the richness of Indian and global cuisines.
